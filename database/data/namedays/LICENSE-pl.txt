Modifications (CC BY-SA 4.0 section 3(a)(1)(B)): the wikitext was parsed into a JSON list per day
(MM-DD). Links, markup and templates were removed; names were normalised; a capitalised parenthetical
was kept as an alternative spelling; lowercase parenthetical qualifiers (for example "w latach
nieprzestepnych") and the line "Wszyscy, w tym:" were dropped. The parser is
scripts/namedays/build-pl.php.
