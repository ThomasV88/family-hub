| work dir → AI CLI | whatever is in the work dir is visible to an external AI provider |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-n1p-01 | Denial of service | HeicConverter heif-dec spawn | mitigate | `ulimit -v` (ai.heic.ulimit_kb), hard timeout with SIGKILL, ImageProcessor decode budget, temp dir removed in finally; the worker is single-concurrency under MEM_AI 768m |
| T-n1p-02 | Elevation of privilege | libheif/libde265 parser bugs | mitigate | Runs only in the ai-worker as a non-root user with the medical volume read-only and no app secrets; argv array, no shell interpolation of file names; decoder only in the ai-runtime stage, not in the web image |
| T-n1p-03 | Information disclosure | EXIF/GPS in the HEIC sent to the AI provider | mitigate | GD re-encode through ImageProcessor drops all metadata; a test asserts no Exif marker or GPS in doc.jpg |
| T-n1p-04 | Information disclosure | doc.heic or the intermediate visible to the CLI, or a stored copy | mitigate | doc.heic is unlinked before the attempt, the intermediate stays in its own temp dir, the test asserts the work-dir listing at spawn time and the unchanged medical disk |
| T-n1p-05 | Information disclosure | decoder stderr/paths in logs or exceptions | mitigate | Output is discarded and the failure is content-free (FailureCode only); AiJobLeakTest stays green |
| T-n1p-06 | Denial of service | 20 PDF pages (time, tmpfs) | mitigate | Separate pages_timeout, the job deadline and min_call guard, tmpfs headroom checked and documented |
| T-n1p-SC | Tampering | apk packages (libheif-tools, libde265 plugin) | accept | Official Alpine repositories through the pinned php:8.4-fpm-alpine base, the same channel as poppler-utils; no npm/pip/cargo installs |
