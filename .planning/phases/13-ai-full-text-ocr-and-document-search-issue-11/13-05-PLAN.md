## Contracts (from 13-04 and the UI-SPEC)

- Props on `Pages/Health/Person.vue`: `search: {enabled, textReliability}`, `documents[].hasText`, `people`, `person`. Search JSON `{items: [{documentId, personId, personName, title, uploadedOn, visitId, visitName, page, snippets: [[{text, hit}]], textComplete, pagesRead, pagesTotal}], hasMore, page}`. Text JSON `{pages: [{page, text}], pagesRead, pagesTotal, complete, language}`; 403 or 404 means "not available" (section disappears silently); DELETE answers 204.
- `useDocumentSearch.js`: state machine `idle | too_short | loading | results | empty | error | throttled`, 300 ms debounce, immediate submit, `AbortController` plus a request counter so only the newest answer renders, `securityFetch('/health/search', {method: 'POST', body: {q, person, page}, replay: true})`, append on Load more, 423 and session expiry through `handleHealthRefusal`, 429 to `throttled` (box usable again after 5 s), state cleared on refusal. It never touches `router`, `history`, `location`, `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or caches.
- `recognisedText.js`: `copyFormat(pages)` returns the whole text with each page after the first introduced by a blank line plus the localised "Page {n}" label (single page: just the text); pinned by a test.
- Copy: every string in the UI-SPEC Copywriting Contract rows that belong to sections 1, 2 and 5 (search label, placeholder, submit, clear, scope, idle hint, too short, results heading with ICU plural in the existing 4-form sk and pl pattern, plain heading with more pages, showing count, load more and its announcement, title-only line, page meta, partial note, uncertain alert, empty state title, body and button, error, load-more failure, 429, recognised text title, disclosure aria label, region aria label, page label, tags, info line, partial alert, nothing legible, being read, being re-read, copy, copy toasts and failure, remove button, confirm title, body and buttons, removed toast, remove failure, text load failure, live announcements) as `health.search.*` and `health.document_text.*` keys, en verbatim from the UI-SPEC, sk and pl from its draft columns.
- Help tips: `health.search` (area health, screen `document_search`, field `search`): what is searched (names and the text AI read), only documents you may open, the words you type are not saved and not put in the address. `health.document_text` (area health, screen `document_text`, field `recognised_text`): what the recognised text is, who can see it (everyone who may open the document), it can contain errors, editors can remove it, backups. Each under 400 characters in en, sk, pl.
- Manual sections (en source): `search` — where the box is and when it appears, two letters minimum, accents and word endings, All people or one person, documents never read by AI are found by name only, results show only documents you may open, what "may contain errors" means, partial reading, words you type are not saved. `recognised-text` — what it is, who sees it (same people as the document, read-only for view-only members), copy, remove (editors; document and accepted suggestions stay; backups keep copies until they expire), partial reading and page cap, deleted with the document, hidden with archived documents and archived people, turning AI off for a person deletes the recognised text of that person's documents (owner default implemented in 13-03).

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-13-26 | Tampering (XSS) | snippets, titles, recognised text | mitigate | interpolation only, segments via v-for to span and mark, no v-html (source scan test), hostile-markup tests |
| T-13-27 | Information disclosure | query and text persisted on shared devices | mitigate | POST body only, no router, history, storage or SW cache (spied in tests), text fetched on expand and dropped on close, no-store responses, autocomplete off |
| T-13-28 | Information disclosure | screen reader or live region reads content aloud unexpectedly | mitigate | live region announces counts and states only |
| T-13-29 | Elevation | remove shown to view-only members | mitigate | button not rendered without edit; server enforces Edit (13-04) |
| T-13-30 | Information disclosure | client infers hidden documents | mitigate | identical empty state for every empty answer; 403 and 404 both make the text section disappear |
