## Decision rules to apply (written before the run; do not adjust after seeing numbers)

- Pre-processing: research "Decision rule" items 1 to 5 as implemented by `EvalDecision` (13-01). `preprocess` is the greedy chain it returns, or `none`.
- PDF scale: 2048 if PDF-2048 beats PDF-1800 on CER beyond the margin with no diacritics or numeric regression, else 1800.
- Pipeline: `two_call` unless entry F1 of P1 is more than 2 pp below P0, then `combined`.
- Seconds per page: the p90 of step-1 transcription wall time per page over all successful stage C and E calls at the chosen arm. `pages_per_call` = the largest N in 1..4 with N x seconds_per_page <= 240 (process timeout 300 s minus 60 s margin). `max_pages` = floor((660 - 60 - 120 - 60) / seconds_per_page), capped at 20; job and process timeouts are NOT raised (owner default).
- Store text: `go` when the typical set (`clean, jpeg, rot3, blur` at the chosen arm) meets CER <= 3 percent, diacritics >= 97 percent, numeric >= 99 percent and search-term recall >= 90 percent; otherwise `go_with_label` (owner default: ship with the "may contain errors" label, no Tesseract, no dropping). `text_reliability` = `reliable` for go, `uncertain` for go_with_label. `no_go` is not an outcome under the owner defaults.
- Evidence field: `no` (owner default).
- If the run stops early (quota) before stage C finished for all six documents, the gate is not decided: Task 2 records the partial numbers, sets `status: incomplete` in the block and the orchestrator re-runs the remaining stages after the quota window resets (`--stage=` resume). Plans 13-06+ are not planned from an incomplete block.
