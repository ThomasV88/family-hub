## Phase Goal

**As a** family member with Health access, **I want to** keep each person's visits, medications, allergies, vaccinations, documents, exports and emergency card in one private place, **so that** our medical history is at hand without any AI while people I may not see stay invisible to me.

<objective>
Make Health reachable and observable without leaking anything: nav link to /health, a static Home card, the "Back to Health" link on the 403 page for /health URLs (UI-SPEC Privacy contract, checker W6), readable medical audit rows for admins, and history clearing when the idle gate locks a session.

Output: nav and Home changes, audit table mapping, confirm-access history clearing, tests.
</objective>
