## Phase Goal

**As a** family member with access to Health or Finances, **I want to** protect my account with an authenticator app or passkey and be asked to confirm it's me after inactivity, **so that** medical and money data stay private even when a password leaks or a device is left unlocked.

<objective>
Close the Google path: Google sign-in must go through the same second-factor challenge, a Google sign-in counts as a confirmation, and members with no password and no factor can get "Sign in with Google again" as their re-auth method on the confirm page, behind the `security.google_reauth` flag (default off) until 06-13 records how Google actually behaves.

## Behaviour contract

- `GET /confirm-access/google` name `confirm-access.google`, `access:authenticated`, `throttle:10,1`: 404 unless `App\Security\ReauthMethods::googleAvailable($user)` (06-04: flag `security.google_reauth` on AND google_id set AND password_set false AND no TOTP/passkey) and Google sign-in is configured; otherwise put session `reauth.google` = {user: id, at: now} and return the Socialite redirect with extra parameters `prompt=select_account` and `max_age=0` [ASSUMED: Google's honouring of max_age/prompt is unverified; 06-13 checks it with the owner].
- `/auth/google/callback` changes from `access:guest` to `access:public`; the controller decides:
  1. Signed in and `reauth.google` pulled from the session, fresh (<= 600 s), for this user, and `ReauthMethods::googleAvailable($user)` still true (re-checked at callback time, so turning the flag off or enrolling a factor mid-flow falls to the failure path): read the Socialite identity WITHOUT calling GoogleLoginService::resolve (no linking, no account creation). Identity id equals the member's google_id -> `SensitiveSession::confirm()`, audit ReauthConfirmed {method: google}, 303 to `SafeIntended::pull()`. Otherwise audit SecondFactorFailed {method: google, context: reauth} and redirect /confirm-access with status `google_reauth_failed`.
  2. Signed in without the flag -> redirect '/' (what the guest rule did before).
  3. Guest: existing flow. When the resolved user `hasSecondFactor()`: put login.id, login.remember = false, login.at = now and redirect to route `two-factor.login` (no Auth::login; the Login event and login_success row fire only when the challenge passes). Otherwise Auth::login, session regenerate, `SensitiveSession::confirm()`, redirect `SafeIntended::pull()` ('/' fallback; keep the welcome toast for 'created').
- password_set: InviteService::accept sets password_set false when the account is created through Google; ResetUserPassword sets it true. Everything else keeps the default true. Existing accounts are handled once by 06-01's migration backfill (PasswordSetBackfill).
- ConfirmAccess.vue: when `methods.google` (true only for an eligible member with the flag on), put a full-page link styled as a secondary button `reauth.google` (href /confirm-access/google, not an Inertia link) next to `reauth.no_method` and "Sign in again" (such a member has no ReauthForm methods, so the link sits in the no-method block; when ReauthForm is rendered it can never have google true); show a danger alert `reauth.google_failed` for flash status google_reauth_failed. With the flag off, the no-method member sees exactly what UI-SPEC Screen 8 specifies: `reauth.no_method` and the single "Sign in again" button.
</context>

| T-06-54 | Spoofing | Google session reuse without fresh login | mitigate | whole fallback behind `security.google_reauth` (default off) until 06-13 records the observed prompt/max_age behaviour [ASSUMED]; even when on, offered only to members without a password or factor (ReauthMethods::googleAvailable, re-checked at callback); flag-off members use "Sign in again" (full sign-out) |
