## Phase Goal

**As a** family member with access to Health or Finances, **I want to** protect my account with an authenticator app or passkey and be asked to confirm it's me after inactivity, **so that** medical and money data stay private even when a password leaks or a device is left unlocked.

<objective>
Put passkeys in members' hands: the Passkeys card on Settings > Security (add, name, rename, remove), the "Use a passkey" option on the sign-in challenge, and the passkey option in the shared confirm-it's-you form.

</objective>

## Endpoint contract (from 06-08)

GET /settings/security/passkeys/options -> {options} | 423 confirm_required when stale; POST /settings/security/passkeys {name, credential} -> 201 {passkey} | 423 confirm_required when stale; PATCH /settings/security/passkeys/{id} {name}; DELETE /settings/security/passkeys/{id} (423 confirm_required when stale); GET /two-factor-challenge/passkey/options -> {options}; POST /two-factor-challenge/passkey {credential} -> {redirect} | 422 passkey_failed | 410 challenge_expired | 429 throttle; GET /confirm-access/passkey/options; POST /confirm-access/passkey {credential, mode: 'page'|'dialog'} -> {redirect} | {confirmed:true} | 422 | 429. Name errors: 422 errors.name = 'passkey.error.name'.
