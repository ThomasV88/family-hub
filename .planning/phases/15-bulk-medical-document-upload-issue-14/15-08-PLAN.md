<objective>
The mandatory user documentation for the feature (CLAUDE.md "User documentation", BULK-09): a new Health manual section in three languages, the privacy rule in "Who sees what", and four contextual help tips placed on the new controls with their registry entries and default texts.

This is a section in the existing Health topic, not a new topic: bulk upload is part of the Health module (rule 3 asks for a new topic only for a new module or Settings section). Phase 14 owns getting-started, shopping and faq; this plan does not touch them.

Content facts to state (must match 15-01 to 15-07): start on a person you may edit with "Upload many documents"; one unfinished batch per person; up to 50 files per batch, 10 MB each, PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC; unfinished documents count toward the 200 documents of a person and are not shown in the person's documents or search until finished; AI reads one document at a time and starts while you still upload; statuses waiting, AI reading, paused until a time when the AI allowance is used up (it continues by itself), ready, failed, done; mark each finding Approve or Reject (and edit approved ones); Continue saves approved findings as records, discards rejected ones, keeps undecided ones for later review on the document, and opens the next document; Re-scan reads a document again (at most 5 times), using brightness, contrast and black-and-white, which change only the copy sent to AI, never your stored document, and are remembered; every re-scan is a new AI run and shows in Settings > AI calls (for administrators); unreadable documents: Try again, Keep without findings, Remove; you can close the browser and continue later on any device; unfinished batches and their unfinished documents are deleted 30 days after the batch was started (finished documents and saved records stay); AI consent for the person and the AI notice apply as for Run AI; turning AI off for the person stops the batch (and removes recognised text as before), turning it on again continues; only members who may edit the person's health records can see and use the batch; batch actions are in the audit log.

Purpose: BULK-09 and the user-facing statement of BULK-08.
