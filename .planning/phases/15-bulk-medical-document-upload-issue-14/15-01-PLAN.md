<objective>
Lay the data foundation of resumable server-side upload batches (GitHub #14) and keep unfinished batch documents out of every list until they are finished.

Owner decisions of 2026-11-18 (15-CONTEXT.md), numbered here for traceability in all Phase 15 plans:
- D-01 one batch belongs to one person (chosen on the person card); no per-document person.
- D-02 batch, per-document status, adjustments and approve/reject decisions live in the database; resumable from another device; unfinished batches and their uploaded files expire after 30 days.
- D-03 "Continue" saves the approved findings of the current document to the records immediately, discards rejected ones, shows the next document; a document can be sent to re-scan at any time.
- D-04 brightness, contrast and black-and-white apply only to the copy sent to the AI; the stored original is never modified; settings are saved with the document and remembered.
- D-05 quota exhaustion pauses the batch and it resumes by itself (Phase 9 park/resume); finished work is never lost.
- D-06 access follows the person's record rules; existing AI consent and the Phase 13 text notice apply; audit events; no document content in logs.
- D-07 reuse the Phase 8/9 run job, review flow and guards and the Phase 13 evaluation; do not rebuild.

Planner decisions for the open points (Claude's discretion, recorded here and repeated in the 15-09 decision record):
- Limits: at most 50 files per batch (config `medical.upload_batches.max_files`, at least the 20 the issue asks for), 10 MB per file (the existing `medical.document_max_bytes`), one open batch per person, and pending documents count toward the existing 200-documents-per-person cap.
- An uploaded file is a normal `medical_documents` row (so the Phase 8 run job, previews and review work unchanged, D-07) carrying `upload_batch_id` while unfinished. Pending documents are hidden from the person page, search, exports and the bulk re-read; Continue clears `upload_batch_id` and the document becomes an ordinary document.
- Expiry is 30 days after the batch was created (fixed date, shown in the UI), for open and finished batches alike.
- Re-scans per document are capped at 5 (`medical.upload_batches.max_rescans`) to protect the subscription quota.

Purpose: every later plan builds on these tables; the visibility filter keeps the person page honest while a batch is open.
Output: one migration, two models, config keys, four query filters, two Pest test files.
</objective>
