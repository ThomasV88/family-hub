## Phase design decisions (planner, recorded for the owner; every later plan follows them)

Decision IDs used across Phase 16 plans (the owner's locked decisions from 16-CONTEXT.md are D-purpose, D-manage, D-privacy, D-enforce; the rest are Claude's discretion):

- **G-01 data model:** new tables `wishlist_groups` (id, name varchar 80, mode varchar 8 CHECK in ('gifts','shared'), created_by_id nullable FK users nullOnDelete, timestampsTz) and `wishlist_group_members` (id, group_id FK cascadeOnDelete, user_id FK users cascadeOnDelete, added_by_id nullable FK users nullOnDelete, timestampsTz, unique(group_id,user_id), index user_id). `wishes.owner_id` becomes nullable; new `wishes.group_id` nullable FK wishlist_groups restrictOnDelete (the group service deletes wishes explicitly so images are removed first); CHECK `wishes_owner_xor_group` = `(owner_id IS NULL) <> (group_id IS NULL)`; index (group_id, received_at). Reason: every existing personal query filters `owner_id = X` and so excludes group wishes without being edited, which is the strongest guarantee that personal lists, children lists and managers stay unchanged.
- **G-02 received_by:** new `wishes.received_by_id` nullable FK users nullOnDelete, written only for group wishes (mark received sets it, unmark clears it) so group members see "bought / received by NAME" (owner's "bought state with the name"). Personal rows are never written with it.
- **G-03 sides:** gifts mode: members are recipient-side (exactly what a recipient of a personal list sees today), every other member with Wishlist View is a giver who sees and reserves like on a personal list. Shared mode: the list is private to its members; members are giver-side (see reservations, organizer and contributor names, can reserve and co-buy) AND editors; non-members get 403 everywhere. This reconciles "non-members reserve in gifts mode" (roadmap criterion 2) with "a non-member cannot read or change a group list" (criterion 4, WLG-06): non-members can never change a group list; they can read only a gifts-mode list, as givers, because that is its purpose.
- **G-04 members:** a member (and an invitee) must be eligible exactly like a wishlist manager: `WishlistManagerSync::isEligible` (can sign in and holds Wishlist Edit). "Invite" adds the member directly (no accept step, like shopping list members); the added member can leave at any time. A member who later loses Wishlist Edit stays in the group read-only; losing Wishlist View hides the whole module as today.
- **G-05 limits (config/wishlist.php):** `group_name_max` 80, `group_max_members` 12, `group_max_per_user` 10 (memberships per person, checked when creating and when adding a member).
- **G-06 no admin bypass:** admin status grants nothing on a group list (same as WishAccess today).
- Later plans: G-07 mode switch, G-08 leaving/removal, G-09 deleting a group, G-10 occasions and reminders, G-11 shopping summary, G-12 UI naming (see 16-02, 16-04, 16-06).
