## Planner decisions implemented here (record in the SUMMARY)

- **G-10 occasions and reminders:** group lists have no occasions of their own. Members' birthdays and name days stay personal, reminders keep going to givers of each person exactly as today (OccasionGivers unchanged). The occasion strip on a group page is the same family strip as on any list. Per-group notifications and recurring group occasions are deferred by the owner.
- **G-11 shopping summary:** "Your shopping" lists the viewer's reservations and shares on group lists as their own section titled with the group name, linking to /wishlist?group={id}&wish={wish}. Excluded: group lists where the viewer is recipient-side (gifts-mode member) and group lists the viewer can no longer see (shared-mode non-member). Personal entries keep the exact current JSON shape; group entries add a `group` {id, name} key and carry `owner` {id: null, name: group name}.
- **Page props:** every /wishlist render adds `groups` (GroupAccess::visibleGroups as [{id, name, mode, is_member}]) and `group_candidates` (GroupAccess::candidates, only when the viewer holds Wishlist Edit, otherwise an empty array). Names only; no reservation data.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| browser -> /wishlist?group= and giving routes | group and wish ids are attacker-controlled |
| giving side -> recipient-side viewers | reservations must never reach gifts-mode members |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-16-20 | Information disclosure | reservation leakage to gifts-mode members through the page, the giving routes or the shopping JSON | mitigate | single branch on GroupAccess::isRecipientSide; GivingGuard 403 before any reservation query; ShoppingSummary excludes recipientSideGroupIds; differential tests here and in 16-05 |
| T-16-21 | Elevation of privilege | non-member reading or reserving on a shared-mode list (IDOR on group/wish id) | mitigate | GroupAccess::canView in ListController and GivingGuard::authorize; 403 identical for unknown ids |
| T-16-22 | Tampering | race: reserving while being added to a gifts group or removed from a shared one | mitigate | assertStillGiverOfWish takes a shared lock on the group row, which every membership/mode change holds exclusively (16-02), then re-reads membership |
| T-16-23 | Information disclosure | stale reservations of removed members appearing in their shopping list | mitigate | shopping query requires gifts mode or current membership; purges in 16-02 |
| T-16-24 | Information disclosure | candidates list exposing members to view-only viewers | accept | names only, the same names WishlistMembers already shows to every wishlist viewer; empty for view-only viewers anyway |
