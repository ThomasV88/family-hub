## Content to cover (English source; sk and pl as drafts for native review)

New section `group-lists` "Group lists" in wishlist.json, placed after `reserve` and before `surprise`:
- what a group list is (one list for several people, for example a couple), where it is (the group-list chips under the person buttons), who can create one (anyone with Edit on the Wishlist) and who can be a member (family members with Edit on the Wishlist who can sign in);
- steps: press **New group list**, type a name, choose the mode, tick the members, press **Create list**;
- the two modes with the exact labels **Gifts from others** and **Shared purchases**: in Gifts from others the family reserves for the list and nobody in the group sees what is reserved or by whom (exactly like your own list); in Shared purchases only the group sees the list and everyone in it sees who reserved or bought what, and can reserve and buy together;
- managing: every member can add, edit and remove wishes and, with **Manage list**, rename the list, change members, change the mode (all reservations on the list are cancelled, you are asked to confirm), **Leave this list** (the last member must delete it instead) and **Delete list** (all its wishes go, cannot be undone);
- a person removed from a Gifts from others list becomes an ordinary giver and can then see its reservations, like a removed wishlist manager;
- limits: up to 12 members per list and up to 10 group lists per person;
- group lists have no occasions of their own; birthdays and name days stay personal;
- received wishes on a group list show who marked them.
Update `lists` (mention the group-list chips), `surprise` (one sentence: the same applies to members of a Gifts from others group list), and the `reserve` paragraph about **Your shopping** (gifts on group lists appear under the list's name).
who-sees-what.json table: add rows "A group list (Gifts from others)" / "Everyone with access to the Wishlist" / "Its members with Edit on the Wishlist"; "A group list (Shared purchases)" / "Only its members" / "Its members with Edit on the Wishlist"; "Who reserved a wish on a group list" / "Gifts from others: everyone except the group's members. Shared purchases: the group's members" / "The person who reserved it". Add a link [Group lists](wishlist#group-lists) in the explanatory paragraph.
faq.json `wish-reserved`: one added sentence about group lists in Gifts from others mode, linking wishlist#group-lists.
Keep the same section ids, section count, row count and links in all three languages. Do not add a new `covers` value (the coverage test accepts only known values; group lists are part of module:wishlist). No string names the app; if one does it must say FamilyHUB.
