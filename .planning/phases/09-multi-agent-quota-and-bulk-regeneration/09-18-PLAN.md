**As a** family admin who runs the AI document reading, **I want to** choose which AI agents read documents and in what order, have runs wait out or route around an exhausted usage limit, and re-read past documents in bulk with a dry run first, **so that** document reading keeps working on our subscriptions while nobody's accepted data is overwritten and no private content reaches me or any log.
