## Planner pre-read of the brand guide (to be confirmed in Task 1)

The PDF (jsPDF, 9 A4-landscape pages) is nine embedded JPEG images, one per page, so it does not need poppler: each `stream ... endstream` whose bytes start with FF D8 FF is a complete JPEG. The planner extracted them and read them. Findings:
- p1 cover; p2 overview: master logo is the F mark plus the word "FamilyHUB" on a peach brush stroke. Principles: clean and modern, scalable vector, high contrast for readability, professional.
- p3 colour palette: PRIMARY #F2963E, SECONDARY 1 #FFE1C8, ACCENT #FFF4EB. (The SVGs also use red #e54738, blue #1c4e9a and coral #e8897a inside the mark only.)
- p4 variations: original on cream, reversed on orange, monochrome white on black, black on cream.
- p5 and p6: digital and print mock-ups (favicon is the coloured F mark).
- p7 typography: Coiny 400 for the wordmark/UI plus a type scale. Not adopted: the owner fixed typography as unchanged (CONTEXT). The wordmark SVG paths are already outlined, so no font is needed for the logo.
- p8 usage: use SVG lockups; keep a clear space equal to the icon height around the logo; use the mono-for-dark asset on dark backgrounds; do not stretch, skew, add shadows or glows, place on busy textures, or change the icon/wordmark relationship.
- p9 licensing: commercial use of the delivered files; no trademark guarantee.
