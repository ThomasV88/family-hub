- A. Target: radio "New list" (name input default recipe title, max 80; optional store `UiSelect` with first option "No store") and "Open list" (`UiSelect` of preview lists, default the most recently changed; disabled with "You have no open list you can add to." when none). - B. "Quantities for" `ServingsControl` copy, default = servings chosen on the detail.
- C. Rows, all included by default: checkbox "Include {name}" (44 px), Name input (max 80), Quantity input (decimal) and Unit select (compact, stacked on phone), match tag: "Matched" (Link icon, catalog thumbnail 32 px through `CatalogImage` from `resources/js/Shopping`, catalog name, ghost "Change" opening a small select of catalog items plus "Add as typed item", searchable with `catalogSearch.js`) or "Not in catalog" (warn, PencilLine). Hint at the top: "Items found in the catalog get their picture. The others are added as typed items."
- Merge hint: when the chosen list has a pending line with the same catalog item: same unit -> "Already on the list: {qty}. - `shoppingQuantity.js` (pure): `toShopping(quantity, unitKey, base, target)` scales with `scaleQuantity`, converts g >= 1000 to kg and ml >= 1000 to l (built-in units only), rounds half-up to 2 decimals with a floor of 0.01; returns `{quantity, unitKey, valid}` where valid is false above 999.99; `convertTo(quantity, fromKey, toKey)` for g<->kg and ml<->l only; `formatForInput(n, locale)`.
- D. Footer sticky: ghost Cancel, primary "Add {n} items" / "Create list and add {n} items" (ICU plural in sk and pl), disabled with 0 rows or any invalid included row (name required, "Enter a quantity greater than 0."). Nothing was added. Try again." with rows intact; 409: "This list was locked in the meantime. Choose another list." selecting the Open list radio and focusing the select; 403: the forbidden text.
