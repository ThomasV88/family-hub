## UI contract (12-UI-SPEC screen 1, sections 7 and 8; UI-SPEC wins over the research)

- Nav: MODULES entry `{ key: 'recipes', labelKey: 'nav.recipes', titleKey: 'module.recipes', icon: ChefHat, href: '/recipes' }` after shopping; `ACCESS_KEY.recipes = 'recipes'`. `ChefHat` exists in `@lucide/vue` (verified in lucide-vue.d.ts).
- BottomNav dense mode (D4): add a modifier class on the list when `modules.length >= 7`: label `font-size: var(--text-xs)`, item padding 2px, gap 0; nothing changes at six or fewer. Implement through a prop or class NavItem already supports, or a scoped deep selector; no new token.
- Overview: title "Recipes" (`--text-2xl`), primary "New recipe" (Plus) linking to `/recipes/create` (12-15 turns it into the choice dialog when import is available), full width under the title on phone. Toolbar: search `UiInput` (Search icon, label "Search recipes", placeholder "Title or ingredient", debounce 300 ms, server-side), always-visible favourites toggle (`Heart`, `aria-pressed`), ghost "Filters" (SlidersHorizontal) with active count opening a `UiDialog` bottom sheet on phone and an inline row from 640 px container: Category (multi, chips), Author (first option "Everyone"), Ingredient input, Show segmented radio All/Mine/Shared with me, Favourites only switch (mirrors the toggle). Active filters as removable `UiChip`s plus ghost "Clear filters". Filters in the query string through `router.get('/recipes', params, {preserveState: true, replace: true})`.
- Grid: container query, 1 column under 480 px container width, 2 from 480, 3 from 840, hard cap 3; gap `--space-4` phone, `--space-6` wide.
- Card (`RecipeCard`): `UiCard`, whole card one link to `/recipes/{id}` (focus ring on the card), photo 4:3 cover from `/recipes/{id}/photos/{mainPhoto.id}/thumb?v={version}` with `loading="lazy"` and `alt=""`, placeholder `ChefHat` 32 px on `--color-surface-2`; favourite `Heart` button (44 px, round surface backing) placed after the link in DOM order but visually over the photo, `aria-pressed`, label "Add to favourites"/"Remove from favourites", optimistic PUT `/recipes/{id}/favourite` with rollback and the toast "Could not save. Try again."; title `--text-base` 600 with a 2-line clamp (the grid card is the only place a title is clamped; the accessible name of the link is the full title); meta line "{n} min · {servings} servings" (omit unset parts, minutes = prep + cook), visibility `UiTag` (Lock "Private" / Users "Shared"), first category `UiTag` plus "+{n}"; for other members' recipes "by {first name}" with `UiAvatar` 24.
- Load more: ghost button, appends through `router.get` with `only: ['recipes', 'hasMore', 'page']` and `preserveState`, keeping already shown cards (merge client-side), `aria-live` "{n} more recipes loaded".
- States: no recipes (ChefHat, "No recipes yet", body, primary New recipe when canCreate), no match (SearchX, "No recipe matches", ghost Clear filters), no favourites ("No favourites yet"), loading (6 `UiSkeleton` cards), error (`UiErrorState` "We could not load the recipes." Try again).
- Texts exactly as the UI-SPEC copywriting tables (en source; sk, pl drafts). Text interpolation only, never `v-html`.
