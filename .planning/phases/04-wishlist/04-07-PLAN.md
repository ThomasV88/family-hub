## Decisions taken here

- Files live on the private `local` disk under `wish-images/{wishId}/{ulid}-full.jpg` and `-thumb.jpg` (the uploads volume of plan 04-02 is mounted exactly there). No public disk, no Storage::url, no symlink.
- The job lives in App\Wishlist\Catalog (covered by the reservation-blind arch rule), implements ShouldQueue and ShouldBeUnique (uniqueId = wish id), tries 1, timeout 60. It runs on the worker container only.
- Image URLs in props: `thumb_url` and `full_url` are `route('wishlist.images.show', [wish, variant])` plus `?v=` the first 8 chars of sha1(path), only when status is ready; `host` is image_source_host for fetched images.
- Image cache header decision (record it in the SUMMARY): the image route answers `Cache-Control: private, no-cache` with an ETag, `Vary: Cookie` and nosniff instead of the UI-SPEC wording "private, no-store". Every view still revalidates, so access is re-checked on each request, while unchanged images cost a 304 instead of a full download on phones.
- Memory budget: production PHP memory_limit is 128M (docker/app/php-prod.ini) and the worker container mem_limit is 160m. GD truecolor needs about 4 bytes per pixel, so the pixel cap is lowered from the research's 16 MP to `image_max_pixels = 13000000` (covers 12 MP phone photos up to 4080x3072, about 52 MB decoded); plan 04-05 creates config/wishlist.php with that value. The pipeline never holds two full-resolution bitmaps at once: decode, resize to the full size, free the source, then rotate/flip the small image.
