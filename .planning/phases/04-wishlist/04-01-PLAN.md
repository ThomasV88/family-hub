## Format facts (from the codebase)

- lang/*.json is nested JSON for vue-i18n. Plurals are pipe segments: en 2 segments (one | other), sk and pl 4 segments in the order one | few | many | other (FORM_ORDER in resources/js/i18n.js). A literal `@` must be `{'@'}`; `|` is always a plural separator; `{` starts a placeholder. No exclamation marks (existing tone tests).
- A JSON key cannot be both a string and an object. The UI-SPEC has two such collisions (`wishlist.received` and `wishlist.delete`), resolved in Task 1.
- Server strings live in lang/*/notifications.php with `:param` placeholders; Laravel `trans_choice` uses 2 forms in en and 3 in sk/pl (see tests/Unit/TransChoiceTest.php: sk 1/3/5, pl 1/2/5/12/22).
- `lang/*/notifications.php` already holds the Phase 3 `reminder_*` keys: append, never change them.
