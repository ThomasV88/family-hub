## Phase Goal

**As a** family member, **I want to** keep a gift wishlist that relatives can reserve from and chip in on, **so that** I get gifts I actually want while I never learn who is buying what.

<objective>
Giver UI for reservations and co-buying: status tags on giver cards, the ReservationBox state machine with the inline share form, contributor list and pledge progress, the conflict alert, and the "Your shopping" summary on giver lists and on the occasions screen.

Purpose: CONTEXT decisions "Any member except the recipient can mark 'I'm buying this'; visible with name to other givers" and "A reserved gift can be opened for co-buying: other givers join with an optional amount; the organizer closes it". Orchestrator decision: view-only members see reservations read-only. Everything here lives only in giver components; OwnListView and OwnWishPanel are untouched.
Output: four new components, three updated components, the occasions page update, tests.
</objective>
